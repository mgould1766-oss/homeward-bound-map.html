Day 1

Buena Park → Oakhurst

~5 hours · Yosemite setup day

A simple first leg that positions everyone close to Yosemite’s south entrance without overloading the day.

Drive

Head north toward Yosemite country

Keep this day easy and practical. The goal is arrival, dinner, and a good night’s sleep.

Dinner

Vegetarian-friendly dinner in Oakhurst

Choose something simple nearby: café, pizza, Mexican, or casual diner-style food.

Stay

Hampton Inn Oakhurst-Yosemite

Predictable, close to the park, and ideal for an early start.

What to know

  • Choose Oakhurst over Madera for a smoother Yosemite morning.
  • Keep park bags packed separately.
  • Early bedtime matters tonight.

Day 2

Yosemite → Tehachapi

Morning Yosemite highlights · Flow-through drive day

A cane-friendly Yosemite highlight reel, then a forward-moving afternoon drive so the trip keeps momentum.

Morning

Tunnel View

The iconic Yosemite view with almost no walking. Big payoff, low strain.

Late morning

Bridalveil Fall

The safer waterfall option. Go only as far as feels good.

Lunch

Base Camp Eatery

Easy seating, predictable food, vegetarian-friendly choices.

Afternoon

Drive toward Tehachapi

Let the kids rest while you make real progress toward tomorrow’s Vegas transit day.

Stay

Hampton Inn Tehachapi

Quiet, practical, and well-positioned for the next leg.

What to know

  • This is not a full Yosemite day.
  • Skip anything that feels like too much walking.
  • The win is seeing Yosemite without exhausting everyone.

Day 3

Tehachapi → Las Vegas

~4 hours · Transit day

A deliberately easy day. Vegas is just a positioning stop, with one fun drive-by moment for the kids.

Drive

Easy desert drive

No need to over-plan this one.

Stay

Hampton Inn & Suites Las Vegas Red Rock

Off-strip, quieter, easier parking, and better for the family rhythm.

Evening

Drive the Strip

One slow pass after dark to show the kids the lights. Stay in the car. No parking, no walking.

What to know

  • This is not a Vegas sightseeing day.
  • Eat near the hotel or bring takeout back.
  • The Strip drive should be short and controlled.

Day 4

Las Vegas → Page via Zion

~5 hours plus scenic time · Unexpected treasure day

The route naturally passes through Zion’s scenic highway. This is a drive-through experience, not a hiking day.

Midday

Zion-Mount Carmel Highway

Drive west-to-east through Zion, including the tunnel and canyon views. Your Lifetime Senior Pass makes this easy.

Lunch

Kanab area lunch

Casual western diner or Mexican food with vegetarian-friendly options.

Stay

Hampton Inn & Suites Page - Lake Powell

Perfect setup for Condor Day.

What to know

  • Skip main canyon shuttles and long hikes.
  • Pull-offs are optional.
  • This is a gorgeous road day.

Day 5 · Condor Day

Page → Navajo Bridge → Page

Light driving · Emotional highlight day

This day is built around the chance to see California condors at Navajo Bridge. Slow down and let this be the moment.

Morning

Navajo Bridge

Flat, manageable walking on the pedestrian bridge. Watch the bridge beams and cliffs for condors.

Bring binocularsCane-friendly

Lunch

Marble Canyon Lodge area

Simple, nearby, and low-stress with vegetarian-friendly basics.

Stay

Hampton Inn & Suites Page - Lake Powell

Return for pool, laundry, rest, and an easy evening.

What to know

  • Do not rush this stop.
  • Morning is best.
  • Horseshoe Bend is optional and probably skippable with the cane.

Day 6

Page → Albuquerque

~5 hours · Pueblos day

A meaningful cultural day centered around Acoma Pueblo, kept flexible and respectful.

Drive

Page toward New Mexico

Open landscape and a steady travel rhythm.

Anchor stop

Acoma Pueblo / Sky City Cultural Center

Visit the cultural center and museum. A guided tour is optional only if energy and walking feel manageable.

Stay

Hampton Inn Albuquerque area

Easy overnight before the route turns north toward Colorado.

What to know

  • Photography rules may apply.
  • This is a living community.
  • The cultural center alone is enough.

Day 7

Albuquerque → Trinidad

~5–6 hours · Bonus Colorado day

The scenery shifts from New Mexico high desert toward Colorado mountain country.

Optional stop

Capulin Volcano

Drive to the top if timing works. Big views with minimal walking.

State moment

Enter Colorado

A new state and a new landscape.

Stay

Hampton Inn Trinidad

Small-town stop that keeps the next day balanced.

What to know

  • Elevation increases.
  • Expect cooler air.
  • This is a transition day, not a packed day.

Day 8 · Cowboy Day

Trinidad → Dodge City

~5 hours · Plains begin

This is Maverick’s cowboy day. Save the outfit for Dodge City and Boot Hill.

Drive

Colorado into Kansas

The land opens up and the sky gets huge.

Main stop

Boot Hill Museum

Old West town energy and the perfect backdrop for Maverick’s cowboy outfit.

Stay

Hampton Inn & Suites Dodge City

Consistent, easy, and right for this stop.

Maverick note

  • Pack cowboy outfit where it is easy to grab.
  • Let him lead the group for a few minutes.
  • This is not just a museum stop. It is a memory.

Day 9

Dodge City → Kansas City

~5–5.5 hours · Diner day

A classic middle-America drive with a diner lunch instead of Panera.

Drive

Kansas plains toward Missouri

Subtle but satisfying landscape change: more towns, more trees, more texture.

Lunch

Classic diner stop

Look for veggie omelets, grilled cheese, pancakes, salads, or sides.

Stay

Hampton Inn Kansas City area

Choose an easy suburban location to avoid downtown stress.

What to know

  • This is a quieter drive day.
  • The diner fits the story better than a chain.
  • Evening should stay simple.

Day 10

Kansas City → St. Louis → Effingham

Arch day · No tram ride

See the Gateway Arch from the ground, take photos, and skip the height anxiety completely.

Main stop

Gateway Arch grounds

Walk near the Arch, look up, take photos, and enjoy the landmark without going inside or up.

Afternoon

Continue to Effingham

This keeps tomorrow from becoming too long.

Stay

Hampton Inn Effingham

Practical, quiet overnight after the landmark stop.

What to know

  • Skip the tram.
  • Thirty to sixty minutes on the grounds is enough.
  • This is a visual landmark day.

Day 11

Effingham → Beckley

~5 hours · Appalachia returns

The road shifts from farmland back toward hills, trees, and mountain scenery.

Drive

Midwest into West Virginia

Terrain changes slowly, then beautifully.

Scenic stop

New River Gorge Bridge

Visitor center and bridge views. Keep it short and easy.

Stay

Hampton Inn Beckley

A good final overnight before the home stretch.

What to know

  • Roads may feel curvier.
  • This day feels like you are getting closer to home.
  • Keep dinner simple.

Day 12

Beckley → Goldsboro

~5–5.5 hours · Home stretch

A simple final day designed to end the trip calmly, not exhausted.

Drive

West Virginia toward North Carolina

Mountains give way to flatter, more familiar roads.

Lunch

Easy diner or sandwich stop

Keep the final day simple and flexible.

Arrival

Goldsboro

Home. The story closes here.

We did it 🎉

What to know

  • No need to over-plan.
  • Skip extra stops unless the kids need one.
  • Let this feel like the closing chapter.
Route note: Hampton Inns are planned wherever possible. Restaurants should be checked closer to travel for current hours and vegetarian options.
